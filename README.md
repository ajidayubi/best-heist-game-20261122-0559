The Meridian Job — a top-down heist game on a casino vault floor. Slip past patrolling guards and sweeping security cameras, time the blinking laser grid, hack the terminal, crack the vault safe, grab the loot and reach the service lift unseen.

## How to play

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Click to toss a beeper and lure a guard (5 per night). Hold E near the hack terminal to switch the cameras off, hold E at the vault safe to crack it. Stay out of the amber guard cones and red camera cones — if anything sees you the alarm meter fills, and at full it trips the alarm. Three alarms and the night is over. R restarts, M mutes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
